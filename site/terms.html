Terms of use

Last updated 7 September 2026

The service publishes mechanical scores for public Lean GitHub repositories. It is provided as-is, without a service level, and may change or stop at any time.

Scores

A grade is not a proof of mathematical correctness, soundness, security, or fitness for purpose. Compare scores only for the same analyzer version. Kernel acceptance and project quality are different properties.

Submissions

Submit only public http or https repository URLs that you are allowed to have fetched and discussed publicly. Analysis executes the project’s build. You ask us to clone that revision, generate a report, and may ask us to publish it in the index.
