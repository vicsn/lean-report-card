Privacy

Last updated 7 September 2026

Lean Report Card is a static website. Published reports are public JSON. Form submissions are posted to a function on this site, which emails them to the maintainers so we can operate the index.

What we collect

If you submit a repository: the repository URL, your email address, whether the request is a rescan, and whether you allow the result to be published on this website. If you use Contact: your message and email. Nothing else is collected from your visit.

What we do not collect

There is no analytics or tracking code on this site. We set no cookies, do not record sessions, do not autocapture clicks or forms, and do not build visitor profiles. Only what you type into a form is sent to us.

Public reports

Analyzer output for a public repository may be published in reports/ and shown in the index. Do not submit a repository if you do not want that result to be public.

Retention and contact

Submissions arrive as email and stay in the maintainers' mailbox until we delete them. Our hosting and email provider is Cloudflare, which processes the submission in order to deliver it. Use the Contact control on the index if you want a submission removed. This notice is for a small research service and is not legal advice.
